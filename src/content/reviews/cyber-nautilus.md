The **Cyber Nautilus SAO** is a gorgeous marine-inspired mechanical add-on designed to plug directly into any standard conference badge. It serves as a proof of concept for elegant, low-profile component layouts.

## Technical Overview
The board taps directly into the 3.3V power rails and I2C lines provided by the host badge's 6-pin SAO V2 connector.

### Core Features:
* **SAO V2 Compatibility:** Keyed 2x3 pin header interface ensures safe orientation and steady power delivery.
* **Chonky Capacitors:** Features exposed surface-mount capacitors designed to mimic the chambered sections of a real nautilus shell.
* **Pulse Mode:** Features an onboard micro-controller that reads ambient chatter on the bus lines to flash its array of amber indicators.

## The Verdict
If you have an open SAO header on your defcon or hackaday badge, this add-on provides an immediate, striking visual boost without overloading your battery budget.
