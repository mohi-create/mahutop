The **Cyber Scarab** by *tilde industries* is an exquisite piece of wearable electronic art. Designed as a mini-hardware badge and wearable pin, it pays homage to ancient Egyptian iconography blended seamlessly with modern cyberpunk aesthetics. 

## Technical Overview
The badge features highly intricate, exposed gold immersion (ENIG) PCB traces that form the geometric armor layers of the scarab beetle. 

### Core Features:
* **Self-Blinking LEDs:** Features reverse-mount RGB LEDs that emit a beautifully diffused glow from the underside of the board.
* **Power Efficiency:** Operates entirely off a single, standard CR2032 coin cell battery.
* **SAO Header Expansion:** Includes standard Simple Add-On (SAO) V2 headers on the back, allowing you to chain it to larger conference badges or power it externally.

## The Verdict
Whether pinned to a hacker con lanyard, a jacket, or integrated into an independent hardware project, the Cyber Scarab stands out as a masterclass in aesthetic PCB design. It balances historical art styles with maker culture perfectly.
